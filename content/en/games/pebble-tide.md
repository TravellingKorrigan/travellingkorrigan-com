Pebble Tide is a bubble shooter on a Breton beach. Pebbles are piled up against the cliff. Touch where a pebble should go, let go, and when three or more of a kind meet, they roll down into the sea.

The pebbles never come down towards you. The tide comes up instead. Every few throws the water rises a little and the korrigan steps back up the beach, because nobody likes wet feet. When there is no room left, a wave catches up. There is no game over: shake your feet dry, then carry on or start the cove again.

## Two korrigans

Play as the korriganez, a female korrigan in a lace coiffe with two copper braids, or as the korrigan in his round Breton hat. Both throw with a wooden slingshot, hurry back from the waves, and sit down on the sand when the cove is clear.

## Eight coves

Each cove has its own shape: a rocky point, an arch, a crooked cliff, a hollow cliff, a cove full of shells. Their names are in Breton, with their meaning beside them. A switch scatters the pebbles anew at every start.

## Made to be calm

The tide counts throws, never time. Put the phone down and nothing moves. Swapping the pebble in hand is free, and in zen mode the tide stays out.

Three difficulty levels, Gentle, Classic and Tricky, change how quickly the tide comes in and how the pebbles are mixed. Every cove can always be cleared. Gentle is the default, and all coves are open from the start.

The six pebbles differ in shape and pattern as well as colour, so you never need to tell colours apart.
